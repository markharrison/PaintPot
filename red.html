A color field guide

Shades of Red

From the richness of burgundy to the warmth of coral, red comes in many moods. Explore a few familiar variations and their hexadecimal color values.

Burgundy

#800020

Rich and refined, burgundy carries the depth of a fine red wine.

Crimson

#DC143C

A bold, slightly blue-toned red that feels dramatic and passionate.

Scarlet

#FF2400

Brilliant and fiery, scarlet leans toward orange with striking intensity.

Cherry Red

#D2042D

Bright and glossy, like ripe fruit at the height of summer.

Brick Red

#B22222

Earthy and grounded, this warm red recalls sun-baked clay.

Coral Red

#FF6F61

A soft, lively red with a gentle pink-orange glow.

Color appearance can vary between screens and lighting conditions.
