A color field guide

Shades of Blue

From the quiet depth of midnight to the brightness of a clear sky, blue comes in many moods. Explore a few familiar variations and their hexadecimal color values.

Midnight Blue

#0B1F3A

Deep and composed, this near-navy shade brings a sense of focus and formality.

Royal Blue

#1E3A8A

A strong, saturated blue that feels confident and classic.

Cobalt Blue

#2563EB

Vivid and energetic, cobalt stands out while staying unmistakably blue.

Azure

#0F9BD7

A bright blue with a gentle cyan note, like sunlight across open water.

Sky Blue

#87CEEB

Light, open, and airy—the color of a calm daytime sky.

Powder Blue

#B7C9E2

A soft, muted blue that makes a soothing backdrop.

Color appearance can vary between screens and lighting conditions.
