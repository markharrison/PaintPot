Shades of Green

From deep woodland tones to fresh mint and lively lime, green comes in many moods. Explore six familiar variations with sample hexadecimal color values and ideas for using them.

Forest Green

#228B22

A rich, leafy green inspired by woodland foliage. Use it for grounded accents and nature-inspired palettes.

Emerald Green

#50C878

A vivid, jewel-like green with a cool undertone. It brings a bright, polished accent to a neutral palette.

Olive Green

#808000

An earthy yellow-green with a muted character. Pair it with cream, tan, or brown for a warm, natural look.

Sage Green

#B2AC88

A soft, grayish green reminiscent of sage leaves. Its restrained tone works well as a gentle backdrop.

Mint Green

#98FF98

A pale, fresh green that feels light and airy. Combine it with white or deeper greens for a crisp palette.

Lime Green

#32CD32

A bright, energetic green with a yellow note. Use it sparingly for playful highlights that stand out.

Shade names can cover a range of colors; these values are examples. Color appearance can vary between screens and lighting conditions.
